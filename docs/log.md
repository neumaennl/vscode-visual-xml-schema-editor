## 2026-11-25

- **Update**: proper support for compositors ([#448](https://github.com/neumaennl/vscode-visual-xml-schema-editor/issues/448)). [Commands](commands.md#compositors): the field that names the compositor is `compositor`, formerly `contentModel`; compositors have commands of their own, `addCompositor` (a sequence or a choice in a sequence or a choice), `removeCompositor` and `modifyCompositor` (kind and occurrences), and `removeGroup` and `modifyGroup` only take group definitions and group references. A new compositor kind takes over the particles of the old one; [Commands](commands.md#conventions) lists the occurrence rules of `all` and the new constraint that a group definition with an `all` is only referenced as the whole content of a complex type, and the compositor of a group definition cannot be removed. [UX concept](ux-concept.md#general) has a Compositor field and says that compositors can be deleted; [Drag and drop](drag-and-drop.md#drop-rules) says "compositor" instead of "content model" and lets sequences and choices be dropped on sequences and choices; [ADR 003](architecture/003-validators-check-executors-assume.md) notes that the compositor commands took over the rules of the group commands for compositors. [Known issues](known-issues.md#an-all-is-not-checked-against-the-derivation): "An all is not checked against the derivation" is new.
- **Update**: [Schema model](schema-model.md), [Testing](testing.md) and [Architecture](architecture.md) name `shared/schemaObjects.ts`, formerly `shared/schemaUtils.ts`, and [Schema model](schema-model.md) names `idStrategy.ts` for `isSchemaRoot()`.
